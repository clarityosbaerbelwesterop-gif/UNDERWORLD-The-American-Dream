- Original, procedural (bpy skin-modifier body + sculpted UV-sphere head); stylized but proportioned: 1.82 m, late 30s, lean-athletic, short dark hair, stubble, scar over the **left** eyebrow, worn leather jacket, grey henley (3 buttons), dark jeans, work boots.

- TD-004: 1 BU = 1 m, origin at the feet on z = 0, transforms applied, faces Blender −Y → Godot +Z. T-pose rest.

## Rig
- `Root` (origin, never translated, no keys) → `Hips` → the 22 required `SkeletonProfileHumanoid` bones + 15 finger bones per hand (Thumb Metacarpal/Proximal/Distal, Index/Middle/Ring/Little Proximal/Intermediate/Distal) = **53 deform bones**. `LeftEye/RightEye/Jaw` not present (optional).
- Bone-heat weights, limited to 4, normalized, 0 unweighted verts; head/hair/eyes rigid to `Head`, buttons to `UpperChest`.
- `SOCKET_RightHand_Weapon` parented to bone `RightHand`, origin = grip point. Verified in Godot: imported under `BoneAttachment3D(bone_name=RightHand)`; in `A_Jason_Aim_Pistol_Idle` its global basis is +Y = up (1.000), +Z = barrel = character forward (1.000), at (−0.053, 1.365, 0.550).

## Open issues / for Bot 1 + Bot 3
1. **RESOLVED (director 2026-11-22): renamed to `A_Jason_Jump_Air`; TD-005 addendum forbids `_Loop` names, uwp error `NAME_ACTION_LOOP`. Bot 3: update `CLIP_NAMES["jump_loop"]` to `A_Jason_Jump_Air`.** Original finding: `A_Jason_Jump_Loop` arrives in Godot as `A_Jason_Jump`. Godot's scene importer treats a trailing `_loop`/`-loop`/`_cycle`/`-cycle` (any case) as a loop hint, strips it and forces looping (verified 4.7.2). `CharacterVisual.CLIP_NAMES["jump_loop"]` asks for `A_Jason_Jump_Loop`, so it will fall back to Idle. Delivered under the REQ name; needs a decision (Bot 1/Bot 3): (a) `uw_post_import.gd` renames it back using the manifest name, or (b) rename the action (e.g. `A_Jason_Jump_Air`) and update `CLIP_NAMES` – Bot 2 can rebuild in seconds. uwp now warns about this (`ANIM_LOOP_SUFFIX`, case-insensitive).
2. ACCEPTED by director (8 f): `A_Jason_Shoot_Pistol` is 8 f = 0.267 s (REQ 0.25 s = 7.5 f, not an integer frame count at 30 fps); `Jump_Start` likewise 0.267 s for "~0.25 s". Recoil peak at f1, settled by f6 – Bot 3 can play at 1.067× if exactly 0.25 s is needed.
