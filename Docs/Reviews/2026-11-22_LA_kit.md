## Reference comparison

No board. The assembled “Calloway Ave” block is a short road with yellow dashes, two simple buildings, three cartoon palms and a few lamp posts floating in a grey void. Facades are single stucco/brick boxes. The shop has a flat awning and punched window holes. The garage is a brown shoebox with a dark door. The palm is a brown stick and a few green cards. The sidewalk/curb almost disappears. The package crate is the most finished piece (wood + straps) and is still a simple box.

This is a valid Phase 1 massing kit. It is not a believable Los Angeles street.

## Defects

1. No reference board for an LA side street / light-industrial block.
2. Buildings are unbevelled boxes with no façade depth, trim, or openings that feel inhabited.
3. Palm tree is a cartoon prop (`SM_LA_PalmTree_preview.png`).
4. Sidewalk and curb have almost no profile.
5. Assembled block is an island in a void: no junctions, alleys, lots or adjacent fabric.
6. No street furniture, signage, road markings beyond centre lines, or environmental storytelling.
7. Kit is incomplete vs the T-ART-03 list (crosswalk, apartment, traffic light, hydrant, trash can, bus stop, fence, LOD1 gaps).
8. No Phase 8 Godot walk-through under gameplay camera.

## Limitations (honest)

Fine as the vertical-slice layout greybox so missions and vehicle tests can be blocked in. The crate can stay as a stand-in pickup mesh until a dressed package exists. Nothing in this kit is production environment art.
