## Reference comparison

The concept shows a grounded, photoreal man in a zipped leather bomber with a ribbed hem and cuffs, faded skinny-to-straight indigo jeans, laced work boots, a skin-fade haircut and a short groomed beard.

The v2 model is a real humanoid, not a stickman, and the outfit *categories* match (brown jacket, grey shirt, dark jeans, brown boots, watch, left-brow scar). That is where the match ends.

- Hair is a ridged, torn plastic shell, not short textured hair with a skin fade.
- Beard is a painted card/shell with a hard edge, not hair.
- Jacket hangs open with a jagged, chewed hem; the ribbed cuff/hem knit of the concept is missing or reduced to a dark band; the collar is crude.
- Jeans are smooth tubes with almost no folds or wear.
- Boots are slip-on blobs with no laces, eyelets, tongue or tread.
- The T-shirt shows a striped moiré in the previews.
- The face has a scar and a plausible identity in three-quarter, but close-ups are waxy, with weak eyelids and hairline gaps.
- The aim pose is stiff; hands do not read as a real grip.

IoU in the mid-0.7–0.8 range is a blockout-to-forms gate, not a production match.

## Defects

1. Hair shell: ridges, holes and a helmet-like cap (`SK_Jason_preview_face.png`, `…_face_side.png`).
2. Beard is a textured card, not groomed hair (`…_face_side.png`).
3. Jacket hem is ragged and open; no convincing bomber rib or zipper (`…_front.png`, `…_3q.png`).
4. Collar / neck intersection is messy from the side and back.
5. Jeans lack folds, seams and wear (`…_front.png`, `…_back.png`).
6. Boots are featureless slip-ons; concept calls for laced work boots (`…_side.png`, `…_aim.png`).
7. T-shirt albedo moiré / stripes (`…_front.png`).
8. Aim pose and hands are not a natural two-handed pistol hold (`…_aim.png`).
9. No character LODs.
10. No Phase 8 in-engine capture under gameplay lighting.

## Limitations (honest)

Useful as the current playable stand-in and as a pipeline demonstration (MakeHuman base, PBR bake path, 53-bone rig, socket). It is not close-up ready and not Rockstar-level. T-ART-01b (hair, beard, jacket hem, boots, LODs, better animation) remains required.

## Verdict

**REJECT**

Not `PRODUCTION-READY`. Do not describe this asset as AAA.
