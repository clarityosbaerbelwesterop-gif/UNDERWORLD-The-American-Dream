## Reference comparison

No board. The silhouette is only vaguely a semi-automatic pistol. The side preview clearly shows a **diamond-plate / tread-plate** material on slide, frame and magazine — a Poly Haven `metal_plate` stand-in, not gun metal. The trigger guard is an open, broken rectangle. The barrel protrudes as a crude cylinder. The magazine and base plate do not sit in a real magazine well.

## Defects

1. No reference board (collect a licensed or original pistol concept; do not copy a real trademarked firearm).
2. Diamond-plate material on the whole gun (`WPN_Pistol_01` side preview).
3. Trigger guard is open / unclosed.
4. Barrel is a sticking cylinder, not flush with the slide.
5. Magazine well and base plate are blockout geometry.
6. No Phase 8 capture in the character’s hands.

## Limitations (honest)

Good enough as a gameplay collider and socket carrier so gunplay can be tested. Not a hero weapon. Material swap alone will not save it; the body needs a real modelling pass.
